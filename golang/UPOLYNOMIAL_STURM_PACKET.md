Actual later Read/Eval checks initialized bound/generation. A zero-count stale view performs no read. Metadata reallocation does not invalidate captured coefficient pointers; coefficient reallocation does. Independent failure accounting includes legitimate sequence coefficients and real manager cache roots before any formatting/Del/Close. No all-or-nothing sequence replacement.
